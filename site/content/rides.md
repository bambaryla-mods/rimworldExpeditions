## Rides

| Ride | When you can pick it | There | Back | What comes home | Arrival |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| On foot | Always | Walking | Walking | What the squad and pack animals can carry | Quiet |
| Drop pods | Enough pods, with launchers and fuel, on the map | Fast; the pods are used up | Walking | What they can carry | Loud |
| Shuttle | Your own shuttle on the map, with fuel for both ways | Fast | Fast | The shuttle's cargo room | Loud |
| Vehicles | Vehicle Framework, and seats for the whole squad | Faster than walking | Driving | The vehicles' cargo room | Loud |

Walking takes as long as a caravan of the same pawns would. Flights are quick and grow a little with distance.

### Quiet and loud

Every ride but walking is loud: it gives the squad away. A loud arrival caps the [stealth](challenges.html#stealth) score at {{ tuning("loudStealthCap") }}, however sneaky the squad is. At a site that leans on stealth, such as the {{ site_link("Exp_ImperialTemple") }}, walk in.

### Armed vehicles

An armed vehicle sometimes helps in a fight. The help depends on how open the site is: a tank does a lot at an open camp and little inside a ruin. It also varies from trip to trip.

### Carrying it home

The [haul](challenges.html#haul) challenge counts the squad's carrying capacity, and a shuttle or vehicles add their cargo room. When there is more loot than room, the squad keeps the most valuable per kilogram and leaves the rest. The report lists what was left behind.

## Food

The squad needs food for the whole trip: there, on site, and back. Each member eats their own diet, pack animals included. The window shows what they need against what they have, and you can't launch while it falls short.

Food counts from what the squad carries first, then from your stockpiles; on the way out they pick up what they need, as a caravan would.

While they are away nothing rots and nobody gets hungry, ages or heals. When they come home, the food for the trip is taken from what they carry, including the share of anyone who died or was captured. Anything beyond that comes back with them.
