## Jobs

Allied and friendly factions near you sometimes need hands. A job pays in silver and goodwill instead of loot, so there is nothing to search for: a success is a success.

Goodwill goes to the faction that asked. Village jobs also earn a little with every friendly faction, because word travels. A Partial pays half the goodwill; a Failure pays nothing.

Some jobs need a hostile settlement near the site too. That is who the squad fights, who takes captives, and whose gear the loot is.

Some factions pay in their own goods instead of silver, worth the same. With Beyond Our Reach, the Solar Republic pays in sunsteel, sunweave and fuel rods once you've reached its tech. Anything too small to make up one unit is paid in silver.

### Raiding a real settlement

The {{ site_link("Exp_SettlementBounty") }} is tied to a real hostile settlement on the world map, in range, with a friendly faction nearby that wants it hit. The squad fights that settlement's people, and a Success or Partial counts as a raid for an ideoligion that cares. The settlement still stands afterwards. If it's destroyed, changes hands, makes peace with you, or you go there yourself while the squad is on the road, they turn back and come home with nothing. Only one offer is ever open on a settlement at a time.

### Freeing people

On a {{ site_link("Exp_SlaverConvoy") }}, the people in the wagons may join you. They're most likely to on a Success, but a Partial frees some of them too.

## Tournaments

The contests are rare, and they are about one champion: the squad's best pawn decides. The prize is small, but the practice is worth a lot: everyone who goes gains three times the usual experience.
