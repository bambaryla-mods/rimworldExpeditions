## Jobs

Allied and friendly factions near you sometimes need hands. A job pays in silver and goodwill instead of loot, so there is nothing to search for: a success is a success.

Goodwill goes to the faction that asked. Village jobs also earn a little with every friendly faction, because word travels. A Partial pays half the goodwill; a Failure pays nothing.

Some jobs need a hostile settlement near the site too. That is who the squad fights, who takes captives, and whose gear the loot is.

## Tournaments

The contests are rare, and they are about one champion: the squad's best pawn decides. The prize is small, but the practice is worth a lot: everyone who goes gains three times the usual experience.
