## Who is held

The **Captured** tab lists every colonist held by another faction: who holds them, for how long, and where. That covers colonists captured on an expedition and colonists kidnapped by raiders in the vanilla game. The game's own rules still apply to them, so a captor may sell them or send them back on a raid.

## Planning a rescue

Pick a captive and press **Plan rescue**. That puts a rescue on the board: at the captor's own settlement if one is in range, or at a holding camp in range if not. A rescue leans on [fight](challenges.html#fight) and [stealth](challenges.html#stealth), so you can storm the place or slip in and out. On a success the captive comes home with the squad.

## Away colonists

Colonists on an expedition are still yours. The colonist bar shows them as away, grouped by expedition, and a colony with everyone away is not over. They don't age, heal or get hungry while away.
