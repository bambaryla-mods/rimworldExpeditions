## What the mod does

Open the Expeditions window from any comms console. It shows your colony's own map: what is around you, a few opportunities at a time, coming and going. Pick one, put together a squad as they stand, and send them off.

They are gone for the trip. What happens out there is rolled off-screen and comes home as a report: what they found, what they could carry, who got hurt, and who did not come back. Sometimes they find nothing.

Gear, skills, bionics and traits all count, and every number is shown before you commit. [How it works](guide.html) explains where those numbers come from.

- [Places to search](finds.html): {{ site_count("finds") }} kinds of site, from an abandoned mine to an ancient mechanitor complex.
- [Jobs for other factions](jobs.html): {{ site_count("jobs") }} kinds of work for allies and villages, paid in silver, goodwill and practice.
- [Prospecting](prospecting.html): ask around on the comms for any item.
- [Captives](captives.html): colonists taken on an expedition, or kidnapped by raiders, can be rescued.
