## Sites for what you believe

With Ideology, some of what your squads hear about depends on your colony's main ideoligion. A raider colony hears about a herd it could steal; a charitable one hears about refugees starving at a crossroads. These offers come and go like any other, but only while the faith fits. Reform the ideoligion so it no longer wants them and they leave the board. A squad already out still finishes the job.

Minority faiths don't count: only the colony's main ideoligion decides what is offered. When a site gives a mood, it goes to those whose own faith wants it, so a member of another faith in the squad doesn't celebrate a shrine burning.

### What they count as

- **Raids.** Cattle rustling, the heretic shrine and the unguarded camp count as a raid on a Success or Partial, the way a vanilla raid does: raiding precepts get their mood and the raid clock resets.
- **Charity.** The alms run counts as charity for every level of the Charity precept, with its own "alms given" mood, as helping beggars or refugees does.
- **Conversion.** A villager who follows the preachers home counts as a new member converted.

### Carrying the alms

The alms run is the one site where the squad brings something. The food to give away is added to the trip's own food, so the launch check covers both, and the medicine is picked up the same way, cheapest first. The opportunity card shows what is needed before you pick a squad. A Success or Partial gives it all away; after a Failure the squad brings it home.

### Captives

From the unguarded camp, the squad brings people home against their will. They travel as prisoners. When the squad gets home, a faith that keeps slaves makes them slaves, as long as a free adult of the squad made it back to take them; otherwise they stay prisoners, for a sacrifice, a meal or whatever else your faith has in mind. If nobody of the squad is left to hold them, they get away.

### Who sees what

Entries from other mods count only when that mod is loaded. "Pacifists" means VME's Pacifist meme and Violence Abhorrent, and Alpha Memes' Vow of Nonviolence and its strict Violence Abhorrent.
