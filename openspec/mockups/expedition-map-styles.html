Map art style: three options

Layout A, map area only. Same world, same markers: Home with its range ring, Old mine (selected), Pirate outpost, Crashed ship, Stalled convoy, Squad 1 walking out, and the camp holding a captured colonist. Everything is generated from the world's own tile data (biome, elevation, hills, rivers, roads), so no hand-painted art is needed for any of them.

1. Vanilla

Biome colors as on the RimWorld world map, flat shading, plain white markers. Feels like part of the game, and looks the most familiar. It's the least distinctive.

2. Tactical holo

Dark map, coastlines and elevation contour lines in cyan, glowing markers and a faint scanline. It reads as a comms-console screen, which fits since the console is where it opens. The biome is only a tint, so it's slightly less readable at a glance.

3. Cartographer

Parchment, ink coastlines, hatched mountains and hand-drawn-style icons. It matches RimWorld's storybook tone and makes the map feel like the colony's own. It needs a parchment texture and some icon art, and it clashes a bit with high-tech colonies.
