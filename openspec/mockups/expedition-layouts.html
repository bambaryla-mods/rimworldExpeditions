Expedition window: three layouts

Same content in each: your flat map centered on the colony, opportunities, one selected PoI with its challenges, squad, ride and odds. Tabs cover Opportunities, Prospect, Active and Captured. Pick the one that feels right, or mix parts. Colors and art style are a separate question.

A. Map first

The map fills the window. A slim list sits on the left, and selecting a PoI opens a details drawer at the bottom. It feels most like "your own map", but the drawer has less room for challenge breakdowns.
